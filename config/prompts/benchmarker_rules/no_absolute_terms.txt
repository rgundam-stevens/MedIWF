<rule>

</rule>

<guidelines>

In some cases, absolute terms are part of a common phrase that does not imply a universal truth such as 'every other day' (i.e. the term 'every' is absolute but the phrase 'every other day' does not imply a universal truth); in said cases return 'pass'.
</guidelines>
