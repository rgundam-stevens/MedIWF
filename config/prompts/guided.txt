Write one single-best-answer multiple-choice question in the style of the USMLE Step 2 CK examination.

Specialty: {specialty}
Topic: {topic}

Requirements:
- A clinical vignette stem describing a patient.
- Exactly five answer options labeled A to E.
- Exactly one correct answer.
- A one-sentence rationale for the correct answer.

Follow these item-writing guidelines strictly (from the NBME Item-Writing Guide and Haladyna et al.):
1. The stem must pose a clear, focused question that a knowledgeable examinee could answer without seeing the options.
2. Include only clinically relevant information in the vignette; no unnecessary detail.
3. Word the lead-in positively. Do not use NOT, EXCEPT, or LEAST.
4. All options must be homogeneous: the same category (e.g., all diagnoses, all next steps) and the same grammatical form, each fitting the lead-in.
5. Keep options similar in length and level of detail. The correct answer must not be the longest, most detailed, or most qualified option.
6. Every distractor must be plausible to an examinee who lacks the specific knowledge tested.
7. Do not use "all of the above", "none of the above", or combinations of other options.
8. Avoid absolute terms (always, never, all, only) and vague frequency terms (usually, often, sometimes) in options.
9. Do not repeat a distinctive word or phrase from the stem in the correct option only.
10. Present numeric options in consistent units and format, in ascending order; present other options in alphabetical order.

Return ONLY a JSON object with this exact structure and nothing else:
{{"stem": "...", "options": {{"A": "...", "B": "...", "C": "...", "D": "...", "E": "..."}}, "answer": "A", "rationale": "..."}}
