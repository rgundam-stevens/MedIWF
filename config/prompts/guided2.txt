Write one single-best-answer multiple-choice question in the style of the USMLE Step 2 CK examination.

Specialty: {specialty}
Topic: {topic}

Requirements:
- A clinical vignette stem describing a patient.
- Exactly five answer options labeled A to E.
- Exactly one correct answer.
- A one-sentence rationale for the correct answer.

Apply the following quality checklist before you answer (each point is a standard from the NBME Item-Writing Guide and Haladyna et al.):
- Options: five choices of the same type (all diagnoses, all tests, or all management steps), phrased in parallel and each completing the lead-in grammatically.
- Options: comparable in length and specificity; never let the correct choice be the longest, the most detailed or the most hedged one.
- Options: no absolute words (always, never, all, only, none) and no frequency hedges (usually, often, sometimes).
- Options: no "all of the above", no "none of the above", no choice built from other choices.
- Options: each wrong choice must be a real contender for someone who does not know the specific fact being tested.
- Options: list numeric choices in ascending order with the same units; list other choices in alphabetical order.
- Stem: the vignette carries only the facts needed to answer; leave out padding.
- Stem: the question is asked in positive terms (no NOT, EXCEPT, LEAST).
- Stem: a well-prepared examinee should be able to answer from the vignette and question alone, before reading the options.
- Stem and key: do not let a distinctive term from the vignette recur only in the correct option.

Return ONLY a JSON object with this exact structure and nothing else:
{{"stem": "...", "options": {{"A": "...", "B": "...", "C": "...", "D": "...", "E": "..."}}, "answer": "A", "rationale": "..."}}
