# NBME items (withheld)

The 667 retired USMLE items with examinee statistics were obtained from the NBME under the data-use agreement of the
BEA 2024 shared task (Yaneva et al., 2024) and are not redistributed. Item-level NBME outputs are withheld;
`outputs/nbme_aggregate_for_figures.json` and the `outputs/*nbme*.txt` aggregates are released and reproduce the figures.
