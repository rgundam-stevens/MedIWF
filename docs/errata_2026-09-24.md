# Errata and clarifications to the dated study documents (24 September 2026)

The protocol, its amendments, the detector changelog, the hand-check log and the rater guide are released as they were
written. This file records what later checks found in them; the documents themselves are unchanged.

1. Amendment 5 (20 September), A23 and A25: "reasoning disabled". The judge calls did not request hidden reasoning.
   GPT-5.4 Mini used none. Gemini 3.1 Pro, whose interface does not allow reasoning to be disabled, returned reasoning
   tokens on every call on the 100 physician-rated items. For Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra the gateway does not honor
   a disable request and those runs did not record reasoning tokens, so whether they reasoned is unknown. The paper
   describes the setting as "not requested". Amendment 1 had already noted that the flagship models reject the request
   to disable reasoning.

2. Protocol (13 September), section "Ethics, employer, and disclosure" and item 2 of the closing list: "ABEM employment
   agreement". The author is not employed by the American Board of Emergency Medicine; he is employed by a contracting
   firm and works at the Board as a software contractor. The agreement referred to is his agreement with that employer.
   The paper's conflict-of-interest statement describes the relationship in those terms.
