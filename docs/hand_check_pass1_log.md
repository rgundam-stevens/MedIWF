# Hand-verification pass 1: procedure log

Rater: the author (RG). Workbook: outputs/verification_sample_pass1_byrule.xlsx (one sheet per rule; built 18 Sep 2026 by scripts/make_verification_helpers.py from outputs/verification_sample_pass1.xlsx, v4 sample, 439 items, detector v1.2). Labelling 19-20 Sep 2026 (UTC dates as saved on disk). Merged by scripts/merge_verification_sheets.py into outputs/verification_sample_pass1_merged.xlsx; analysed by scripts/analyze_verification.py -> outputs/analysis_verification_v1.txt.

Blinding: the rater never saw detector flags. The assistant checked each sheet for completeness and format after the rater declared it finished and ran arithmetic consistency checks that use only the grey helper cells (counts and word lists computed from the item text), never the detector output. No item-level comparison with the detector was shown to the rater before the analysis. (The rater's own pass 2 was later replaced by independent raters, Amendment 5 A22; the independent raters will not see the detector output or the author's labels.)

Sheet-by-sheet record:
1 negative_stem: all 439 typed by the rater; 8 notes (stems with no question sentence; see missing-lead-in observation).
2 meta_option: all 439 typed (rows 1-83 were found blank on the first check and typed afterwards).
3 combination_options: all 439 typed.
4 longest_option_key: all 439 typed; the arithmetic check found 9 rows disagreeing with the grey-cell numbers (7 typed 1 where the key did not exceed 1.25 x the longest distractor, 2 typed 0 where both conditions held); the rater re-read them and corrected all 9 to the arithmetic. Equality (key exactly 1.25 x) was clarified as 0 (strictly more than), matching detector code.
5 absolute_terms: all 439 typed; 1 note. Exemptions judged by the rater; no row marked 1 without a listed word.
6 vague_terms: the rater filtered the sheet for the listed words and typed 1 on 4 rows; the remaining 435 rows were set to 0 by script at the rater's instruction (closed word list, no exemptions). Check: the 4 rows are exactly the rows where the helper found a listed word.
7 clang_cue: 93 rows had at least one candidate word (a key word also in the stem and in no distractor); the rater judged each candidate as distinctive or generic and typed 1/0; the 346 rows with no candidate were set to 0 by script (the rule cannot apply). Nine candidate rows left blank were reviewed and typed by the rater; the rater standardised repeated words (serum-only rows 0; beta-hCG, stress, oxygen rows 1). 17 notes on borderline words (e.g. school/play, childhood, acute, pressure, left, oral, behavior). Standard applied: words naming a drug, disease, organism, body part or region, finding, test, procedure or gene count; setting, time, direction, route and degree words and words repeated from the question sentence do not.
8 grammatical_cue: the rater read all rows and typed the 10 rows where the key is a full sentence (1 on row 402, 0 on the 9 where a distractor is also a full sentence); the other 429 rows were set to 0 by script at the rater's instruction (no question ends in a/an; key not a sentence).
9 nonparallel_options: the rater read all rows and typed 1 on the 35 rows meeting a condition; 404 blanks set to 0 by script at the rater's instruction; 3 'category mix' notes (rows 33, 36, 69). The assistant's independent candidate list for category mix (outputs/category_mix_candidates_assistant_2026-09-20.json, 3 rows: 33, 36, 69) was given to the rater as reference after the rater had started the sheet; the notes are the rater's.
10 overlapping_options: the rater typed 1 on 33 rows; 406 blanks set to 0 by script at the rater's instruction. Check: the 33 rows are exactly the rows where the helper found a containment pair.

Rule difference recorded before sheet 7 was labelled: the rater guide's clang rule (one distinctive stem word in the key and in no distractor) is looser than the detector's (an exclusive shared word AND either two such words or key-stem overlap at least twice the best distractor's). Human positives are therefore expected to exceed detector positives for this rule; recall is interpreted accordingly.

Correction after the first analysis (20 Sep 2026, 19:00-19:40 UTC): the item-level comparison with the detector (made only after the rater's pass 2 had been replaced by two independent raters, so the rater's own second reading is no longer planned) showed that the helper's singular/plural folding had missed '-ies' plurals (arteries/artery, ovaries/ovary), so on three rows (16, 143, 215) the rater had not been shown a legitimate candidate word. The helper was corrected (make_verification_helpers.base: -ies -> -y; -xes/-zes/-ches/-shes/-sses -> drop es; other -s -> drop s; still no -ing/-ed folding, per the guide), the Rule 7 cells were refreshed in place without touching any answer (backup: outputs/_to_delete/verification_sample_pass1_byrule_backup_before_clang_refresh.xlsx), exactly three rows gained a candidate, and the rater re-judged those three (all 1). Merged and analysed again.

Result (pass 1, whole-corpus design-based estimates): see outputs/analysis_verification_v1.txt. After the correction, clang_cue precision 0.79 [0.68, 0.89], recall 0.52 [0.42, 0.66], sample kappa 0.70; the other rules unchanged. Composite over the ten hand-checked rules (scripts/analyze_verification.py, composite section added 21 Sep): precision 0.95 [0.91, 0.98], recall 0.79 [0.70, 0.88] (weighted prevalence detector 18.4%, human 22.1% [19.5, 24.9]); without clang_cue precision 1.00 [0.99, 1.00], recall 0.97 [0.92, 1.00] (12.7% vs 13.1%). An earlier ad hoc figure of 0.93 / 21.7% quoted here before the script was extended is superseded. The clang disagreements are (a) 21 items the rater flagged on a single specific echoed word that the detector's two-word-or-double-overlap criterion does not flag (9 in the unflagged stratum), and (b) 12 detector-positive items the rater judged generic or definitional (-ing/-ed forms, injury, pressure, acid, formula, hormone, regular, school/play, childhood). The author's pass 2 (150 items) was dropped on 20 Sep 2026 in favour of independent raters on the full sample (Amendment 5, A22); scripts/make_pass2.py is retained but unused.

Rater's statement, recorded 22 September 2026 (after the referee reports on v0.6): on every sheet the grey helper columns were kept collapsed while labelling and were opened only after the sheet had been filled in, for a quick self-check; every label was therefore first made from the item text alone, including the clang-cue judgments. The arithmetic checks described above were run after the columns had been opened. Consequences for the record: the nine longest-option cells (sheet 4) and the nine blank clang-cue cells (sheet 7) were re-read and typed after the helper columns were visible; the by-eye longest-option labels before reconciliation agreed with the detector on 48 of its 50 positives and marked 7 further items (unweighted sample precision 0.96, recall 0.87); the overlapping-option (sheet 10) and vague-term (sheet 6) labels agreed with the computation on every item before any reconciliation. The manuscript (v0.8.5, where the passage is kept to the essentials: labels from the item text, nine longest-option labels corrected after comparison with the rule's computation) and the referee triage carry this correction; the earlier description of the arithmetic rules' check as an "implementation check" was the log-keeper's assumption, not the rater's procedure.
