# MedIWF Protocol Amendment 4 — 17 September 2026

Written after an independent second audit (eight reviewer agents: statistics, design, code, two numbers checks, literature,
ethics, writing; reports in docs/reviews_2026-09-16/) and before any hand-verification or physician main-set rating had
been analysed. Everything below was applied on 17 September 2026; the v1.1 flag files and the v3 hand-check sample are
archived, not deleted.

## A15. Detector v1.2
Seven defects found by adversarial testing (docs/detector_changelog.md). All corpora and the NBME items re-scored. No estimate
moved by more than rounding (largest change: NBME any-flaw 17.1% -> 17.5%, four clang-cue cells).

## A16. One composite definition
`src/mediwf/rules.py` defines CORE (14 rules; the primary outcome), SENSITIVITY (11) and VALIDATED (10, the rules with
BenchMarker counterparts). Every analysis script imports it. Two scripts had used other definitions (`analyze_flags.py`
counted option_length_outlier; `nbme_psychometrics.py` used six rules); their outputs are regenerated. The stale
`outputs/nbme_aggregate_for_figures.json` (v1.0 numbers) is regenerated by `make_figures.py` and now documents its definition.

## A17. Analyses added (all released as scripts with outputs)
- `composites.py`: prevalence and guideline OR under CORE, SENSITIVITY, VALIDATED and first-repetition-only; share of flagged
  items flagged only by unvalidated rules; NBME rates under each composite.
- `model_contrasts.py`: RQ2 model differences as deviations from the 15-model mean (replaces the post-hoc reference model).
- `model_level.py`: random-effects pooling of per-model guideline ORs with I^2 and prediction interval; the reasoning arm
  summarised over its four models; model-level intervals for the tier rows; topic-cluster bootstrap intervals per cell.
- `provider_sensitivity.py`: serving-provider mix per model and arm; estimates with secondary-provider items excluded.
- `rationale_key_check.py`, `near_duplicates.py`, `costs.py`, `score_calibration.py`, `check_content_rating_selection.py`:
  numbers the manuscript had quoted from scratch work now come from released scripts.
- `nbme_comparison.py`: LLM vs NBME prevalence under each composite, stem-length-adjusted; absolute-terms word and
  key/distractor tabulation.
- `validate_benchmarker.py` v2: duplicate label rows collapsed; health-sciences block reported; output file written.

## A18. Hand-verification sample re-drawn (v4)
The v3 frame had silently lost 4 of 12,000 items (records not re-parsed with the current validator). The v4 sample is drawn on
the whole corpus with detector v1.2 (seed 20260917): 439 items in 8 mutually exclusive strata (populations sum to 12,000).
`analyze_verification.py` now maps pass-2 ids to pass-1 ids (the v3 code would have paired the wrong items). The rater
guide is re-issued as v3 to match detector v1.2 (v2 described rules 3, 4, 8, 9 and 10 as they were in v1.0). Labelling had
not started.

## A19. Physician calibration: what was fixed in advance and what was not
The guide sent to the raters stated the qualitative pass criterion. The numeric threshold ("at least 6 of 8 planted defects")
was written into Amendment 2 at 00:54 UTC on 16 September, after rater 1's sheet (returned 16:04 UTC on 15 September) had been
scored by hand as 6 of 8. `score_calibration.py` now records the scoring rule (detection = primary question answered
no/unsure) and reports both raters under it and under a strict reading. The manuscript says this plainly. Both raters were
paid for the calibration set irrespective of outcome.

## A20. Manuscript changes (v0.4)
Validation section rewritten (which rules have external evidence; health-sciences block; validated-only composite);

## A21 (18 September 2026): hand-verification workbook with computed helpers
Before any hand-verification label was entered, the pass-1 workbook was rebuilt as outputs/verification_sample_pass1_simple.xlsx (scripts/make_verification_helpers.py): the same 439 items, the same ten label columns and notes, plus grey helper columns computed from the item text by an independent script that does not import the detector: option character and word counts, the Rule 4 arithmetic (1.25 × the longest wrong option), options starting with a number, full-sentence options, the word-count spread ratio, occurrences of the listed sweeping and frequency words, key words that also occur in the stem (with the wrong options that contain them), whole-text containment pairs and whether the question sentence ends in a/an. The helpers show counts and candidates, never the detector's flags; every 1/0 label is entered by the rater. The rater's working copy is outputs/verification_sample_pass1_byrule.xlsx, the same content arranged as one sheet per rule (all 439 items on each sheet, one answer column, only the fields that rule needs; the key letter appears only on the sheets for rules 4, 7 and 8, so the other rules are judged without knowing the key), which the rater completes one rule at a time; scripts/merge_verification_sheets.py folds the ten answer columns back into the flat layout for analysis. Pass 2 (make_pass2.py) produces the same two layouts for its 150 items with new ids. Rationale: the rater found the arithmetic in Rater Guide v3 error-prone; the guide already permitted spreadsheet formulas for the word-count ratio. This is recorded as a procedural detail, not a change to any rule. Pass 1 was completed 19-20 September 2026; the sheet-by-sheet procedure, including the sheets on which blank rows were set to 0 by script at the rater's instruction and the arithmetic reconciliation on the longest-option sheet, is in docs/hand_check_pass1_log.md. Before the clang sheet was labelled it was recorded that the guide's one-word clang rule is looser than the detector's two-word-or-double-overlap rule (docs/hand_check_pass1_log.md).

## Deviations from the protocol, consolidated
1. Fixed-effects regression with topic-clustered errors instead of the planned mixed-effects model.
2. Pre-specified two-way interactions replaced by separate per-model estimates.
3. Only BenchMarker among the planned external corpora was used.
4. Hand-verification sample changed from a simple random sample of 300 to an enriched sample of 439 (Amendment 2), re-drawn in v4.
5. Tier comparison moved to the model level (Amendment 2).
6. The planned human-written comparison set replaced by the NBME items.
7. A12–A14 of Amendment 3 (flagship reasoning, temperature, stronger judges) not run.
8. Open-weight models run through hosted providers rather than locally.
9. The protocol was written before generation but not registered; a retrospective time-stamped deposit is made instead.
